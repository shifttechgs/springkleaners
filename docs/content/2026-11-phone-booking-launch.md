# Content: faster phone booking (November 2026)

Client-facing posts about the booking changes that went live on 19 November 2026 (commits `2a2ffd9`, `d039e83`):
the app-style phone homepage with instant prices and weekend slots, and WhatsApp buttons that open with the
message already written.

Talk about what clients feel, not what changed behind the scenes. The phone booking is the headline; SEO, tests,
tracking and the photo-consent checkbox aren't worth announcing.

## Before you post

- **Days:** booking is **Saturdays and Sundays only** (`Booking::BOOKABLE_DAYS`). These posts say "weekend slot".
  The queued "every-day scheduling" post in [`gbp_posts.md`](../gbp_posts.md) contradicts this, so don't publish it
  until every-day booking is actually switched on.
- **"About a minute"** is the same promise the site makes ("Your price in a minute"). Keep them matched.
- **No prices** on purpose, so the posts can't go out of date. If you add one, check it against the live site.
- **Photos:** real team or job photos only, never stock (see [`design.md`](../design.md)). Best of all is a
  15-second screen recording of booking on a phone: tap a tile, step through, reach "Booking request sent!".
